# Code of Conduct

AxiomNode Lab projects are collaborative technical spaces. Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, review ideas on their technical merits, and make room for contributors with different experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific and constructive when reviewing code or proposals.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Give credit for contributions and prior work.
- Keep security reports and private project information confidential when required.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Assume good faith initially, but respect maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, and repository rules.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action when behavior materially harms the project or its contributors.

For conduct concerns that should not be discussed publicly, contact an organization owner or repository maintainer privately.
